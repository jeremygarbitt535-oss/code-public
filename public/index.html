How Venmo Arbitrageur Works

Venmo Arbitrageur is a calculator and guide. Enter a cashback offer, referral bonus or transfer fee you see in your Venmo or Cash App app and it shows the net value after fees and a risk adjustment.

  • Compare standard (free) vs instant transfers (Venmo instant: 1.75%, min $0.25, max $25)
  • Net out referral bonuses and cashback against any fees
  • Free: 3 calculations per day · Premium ($19 one-time): unlimited calculations, CSV export and the savings guide
